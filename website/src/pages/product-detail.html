Technical Specifications

  • Sensors

    A multi-sensor stack that captures the core signals needed for sleep, recovery, and daily trends.

    • Optical PPG LEDs for pulse and related trends
    • Skin temperature sensor for night-to-night variation
    • 3-axis accelerometer for movement and sleep-wake

  • Compatibility

    Built to fit into your current setup with a simple app experience and modern device support.

    • iOS 15+ and Android 10+
    • Apple Health and Google Fit support (optional sync)
    • Supports metric and imperial units of measurement

  • Weight & Dimensions

    Low-profile proportions designed for comfort and consistent wear.

    • Width: 7.9 mm
    • Thickness: 2.9 mm (approx. wedding band profile)
    • Weight: 3.3 to 5.2 g (varies by size)

  • Battery Life

    Designed for multi-day wear so your trends stay consistent without frequent charging.

    • Battery life: 5 to 8 days (typical use)
    • Charge time: 20 to 80 minutes (varies by level)
    • Charging: USB-C powered dock

    Premium, skin-friendly materials built for everyday wear and long-term comfort

    • Titanium shell with a smooth, non-allergenic interior
    • PVD finish options for color durability
    • Water resistance: up to 100 m

  • Connectivity

    Reliable syncing with low power connectivity, plus quiet updates in the background.

    • Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) for efficient syncing
    • Automatic firmware updates via the app
