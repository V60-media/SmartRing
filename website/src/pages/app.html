What We Track

Track all of you. Not just your steps.

From morning to midnight, every signal your body sends has a home.

  • Step on the scale. Let the trends speak.

    Weight, body fat, muscle mass, BMI. Tracked daily, surfaced as a trend.

  • A quiet read on your heart, every day.

    Blood pressure, resting rate, variability. Tracked at home, surfaced in the app.

  • Glucose, hemoglobin, oxygen. Your blood picture, at home.

    Use Hema One to see what once required a full lab visit.

  • A safe place to check in with yourself.

    Track moods, write privately, notice patterns. Locked behind Face ID by default.

  • Your cycle, tracked within your full health picture.

    Phases, symptoms, and fertility. Connected to how you sleep, move, and feel.

  • Activity and recovery, read as one connected picture.

    Workouts, steps, and deep sleep tracked together because they shape each other.

  • Meals, water, fasting. Nutrition made honest and simple.

    Barcode scanner, manual logging, a recipe library, and a full fasting planner.
