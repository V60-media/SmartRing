    How ring sizing works

    Ring PRO uses custom sizes, distinct from standard smart ring and market sizes. A precise fit is essential for accurate data tracking and comfort.

    1. Step 1: Order your Ring One

      Select the 'Free Sizing Kit' option when you order your Ring One. You're purchasing the Ring One, but we’ll send you the sizing kit first at no extra cost.

    2. Step 2: Sizing kit delivery

      Most orders are processed and delivered within 3 days. This kit includes sample ring sizers to help find your ideal fit.

    3. Step 3: Find your perfect fit

      Test the sample rings on your preferred finger. We recommend the index finger for optimal performance and accuracy. If you prefer another finger, the middle and ring fingers are good alternatives.

    4. Step 4: Submit your ring size

      Confirm your size by visiting the link on the sizing kit’s packaging, and we will ship your Ring One to you. Feel free to share your sizing kit with friends and family.

    * Choosing the right Ring One size is essential for comfort and accurate readings. Ring One sizing is unique in comparison to the US Standard.
